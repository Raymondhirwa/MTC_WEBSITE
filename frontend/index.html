Our Services & Educational Mission

As a premier Technical Secondary School, our primary aim is to deliver high-quality, comprehensive education across all our specialized departments. Whether you are coding in ICT, constructing in B.D.C, cooking in FBO, diagnosing engines in Automobile Technology, or managing finances in Accounting, we bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world career execution.

Multi-Disciplinary Training

We offer a perfectly structured pathway from Level 1 short courses to Level 5 advanced training, along with S4-S6 secondary education, ensuring practical mastery across every single department.

Modern Workshops & Labs

Gain daily hands-on experience in fully equipped computer labs, professional kitchens, automotive garages, and construction sites designed to perfectly simulate actual industry working environments.

Expert Field Instructors

Be guided daily by an elite team of certified software developers, master mechanics, professional chefs, and seasoned engineers dedicated to unlocking your ultimate technical potential.

Direct Industry Employment

Through our robust institutional partnerships, we provide immediate pathways to internships, apprenticeships, and jobs, transforming secondary students into highly sought-after professionals.

Building Technology (B.D.C)

Welcome to the foundation of future infrastructure at MTC! Our Building Technology (B.D.C) department is the ultimate cornerstone of our institution, robustly dedicated to molding aspiring builders, architectural draftsmen, and professional site managers. We provide a deeply immersive, hands-on learning environment guided by highly seasoned construction professionals. We don't just teach you how to assemble materials; we empower you to construct sustainable, environmentally friendly, safe, and wildly innovative structures. At MTC, our BDC students are trained to be the visionary architects and master builders who will boldly define the skylines of tomorrow and lead rapid modernization.

Comprehensive B.D.C Training

Step into the thrilling world of tangible creation and physical transformation. Our Building Technology curriculum takes you on an extensive journey from precise architectural drafting and fundamental masonry, directly into advanced site management and complex civil works. You will spend countless hours in our massive construction workshops, flawlessly translating complex blueprints into stunning physical reality. Throughout your studies (Level 3 to Level 5), you will master the intricate details of material science, structural integrity, strict safety protocols, and modern 3D architectural software. You will learn to measure, cut, plot, construct, and verify with uncompromising precision. Whether you are laying the very first brick or managing the logistics of a large-scale commercial project, this program guarantees you will graduate as a highly skilled, confident, and industry-ready engineering professional fully equipped to conquer modern urban development challenges.

Guided by industry-seasoned master mechanics, you will learn to diagnose, repair, and innovate on a variety of vehicles. From traditional combustion engines to the latest hybrid systems, we empower you to drive the automotive industry forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

When does the next intake start?
Our next intake typically begins in early September. We recommend applying at least one month in advance to secure your spot and complete all documentation.
Do you offer boarding facilities?
Yes, MTC provides safe and comfortable boarding facilities for both male and female students coming from outside Muhanga district.
What are the requirements for the Automobile department?
For the Advanced Level (L3-L5), you need O-Level completion certificates. For the short courses, we require basic literacy and a passion for mechanics.
